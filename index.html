Ways to work together

Three ways to work with us. Pick what fits.

Every engagement starts the same way: a written scope and a named person you can call. The only difference is the shape of the commitment — choose the one that matches how your business actually runs, and switch later if life changes.

Project

Good for: one defined job with a deadline — a rebrand, a new site, a launch.

  • Fixed scope and fixed price, agreed before work begins
  • A milestone schedule with built-in review points
  • Everything handed over — files and accounts — when it's done

Retainer

Good for: ongoing marketing that needs steady hands month after month.

  • A set number of monthly hours, used across any of the four areas
  • Priorities reviewed together each month
  • Effort shifts to wherever your business needs it most

Embedded

Good for: teams that need a marketing department before they can hire one.

  • We work as your in-house team, on your tools
  • A weekly rhythm with your leadership
  • Built to hand off cleanly when you hire your own people

Our promises

Four things we promise every client.

Full-service shouldn't mean full of surprises. These promises apply to every engagement, in every model, and they're written into our agreements — not just this page.

They exist because we've watched what happens without them: owners locked out of their own ad accounts, reports built to flatter, and budgets spent where the agency's margin was best rather than where the client's return was. You deserve better, and it's not hard to give.

  • Your accounts stay yours: ad platforms, domains, and analytics are registered to your business from day one.
  • Ad budgets pass through at cost; our fee sits on its own line, never hidden in the spend.
  • Reports tell you what happened — including what didn't work and what we're changing because of it.
  • No long lock-ins: retainers run month to month after an initial term we state up front.
